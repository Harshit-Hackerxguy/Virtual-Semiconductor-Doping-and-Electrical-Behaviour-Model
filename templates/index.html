Educational note

Conductivity vs breakdown trade-off

Higher doping

Higher doping usually increases conductivity, narrows depletion width, lowers breakdown voltage trend, and makes Zener behavior more likely.

Lower doping

Lower doping usually decreases conductivity, widens depletion width, raises breakdown voltage trend, and makes Avalanche behavior more likely.

Honest scope

This model gives educational trends and categories, not exact semiconductor device ratings.
